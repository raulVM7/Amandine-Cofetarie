About us
A small kitchen, two ovens, and time.
Amandine began as a corner shop with a marble counter and one recipe book. Three decades later the method has not changed: laminated by hand, rested overnight, finished to order. We keep the menu short so every item is worth the room it takes.
— Hélène Roux, head pâtissière
